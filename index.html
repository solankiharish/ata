A Family Business, One Stone at a Time

KK Ata began with a simple belief — that flour should be as pure as the wheat it comes from, ground the way our grandparents ground it, without shortcuts.

Why We Still Use the Chakki

Modern roller mills grind wheat fast, but the heat generated strips away natural oils and nutrients. Our traditional stone chakki grinds slowly and cool, keeping the bran, germ and fibre exactly as nature intended.

Every batch is milled fresh after an order is placed — never sitting in a warehouse for weeks before it reaches you. That's the difference between "packaged atta" and genuinely fresh atta.
